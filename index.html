Support

YourWay is a free iPhone app for getting around London, built around accessibility: step-free routes, lift information, quieter journeys and main-roads walking.

For help, feedback or to report a problem, email AMDeveloper@proton.me. If you can, include your iPhone model, iOS version and a screenshot.

Common questions

“My location” or “Near me” doesn’t work
Check that location is allowed for YourWay in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services, set to “While Using the App”. You can always type a station, postcode or place instead.
Can I rely on the lift information?
Use it as a guide, not a guarantee. Lift faults only appear once staff report them, and live lift status isn’t available for every station. Before travelling, check with the train company, and book Passenger Assist or ask TfL staff for Turn Up and Go help if you need it.
Why are some walking routes longer with main roads on?
Main-roads walking avoids alleys and footpaths and prefers lit streets, which can add a few minutes. It keeps walks on busier roads but can’t guarantee a street is well lit or feels safe.
Where does the travel information come from?
Powered by TfL Open Data. Contains OS data © Crown copyright and database rights 2016 and Geomni UK Map data © and database rights [2019]. Live departure and station information from National Rail. YourWay isn’t affiliated with or endorsed by TfL or National Rail.
