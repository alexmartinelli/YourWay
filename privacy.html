Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

YourWay collects no personal data about you: it has no accounts, keeps no journey history and uses no analytics or tracking.

YourWay is a free iPhone app for planning journeys and checking live departures in London. It is run by an independent developer (“the YourWay developer”), who is responsible for this policy. If you have any questions, contact AMDeveloper@proton.me.

This policy explains what happens to information when you use YourWay, including the outside services the app contacts to show you travel information.

What YourWay collects

YourWay does not collect, store or share personal data on any server of its own. It has no sign-in, no adverts and no analytics.

Your settings are saved only on your iPhone. They include the lines you watch, your saved stops, your colour and plant choices, and your travel options such as step-free access, walking pace, crowding and main-roads walking. They also include a copy of station accessibility details, kept so the app doesn’t have to fetch them repeatedly.

YourWay does not keep a record of your journeys, searches or locations. Deleting the app deletes everything it has stored.

Services the app contacts

To show travel information, YourWay sends requests directly from your iPhone to the services below, only when you use the related feature. Each request contains only what that service needs. Like any website, each service can see your phone’s internet (IP) address when it is contacted. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it.

YourWay does not sell or share information with anyone else.

Your location

YourWay uses your location only when you tap “My location” or “Near me”, and only with your permission. iPhone asks for that permission the first time, and you can change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services.

Your location is used once, to start a journey from where you are or to find nearby stops. It is sent to TfL for that search, and to the walking route service if main-roads walking is on. YourWay never stores your location or tracks it in the background.

If you’re testing YourWay through TestFlight

Apple’s TestFlight service automatically sends crash logs and usage data about the beta app to Apple and to the developer, and testers can’t switch this off. This can include information about your device, such as its model and iOS version. If you send feedback or screenshots through TestFlight, the developer receives those too.

The developer uses this information only to fix problems and improve YourWay, and will not share it. Apple handles it under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Your rights, children and changes

Under UK data protection law you can ask what personal data is held about you, and ask for it to be corrected or deleted. YourWay itself holds none, but you can contact AMDeveloper@proton.me about any TestFlight data or feedback. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

YourWay is not intended for children under 13.

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear at this address with a new date at the top. Questions are welcome at AMDeveloper@proton.me.
